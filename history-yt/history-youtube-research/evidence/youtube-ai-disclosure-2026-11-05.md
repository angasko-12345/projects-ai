# YouTube: Disclosing use of GenAI content
Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491 (fetched 2026-11-05)

Requires disclosure when realistic GenAI content: makes a real person say/do what they did not; alters footage of a real event/place; generates a realistic scene that did not occur; creates music that is the main focus.

Does NOT require disclosure: non-realistic AI animation in a fully animated video; minor edits; production assistance (outline/script/title/thumbnail/infographic), captions, upscaling, cloning your own voice.

Historical relevance: photorealistic reconstructions of real battles/events DO require the AI use disclosure; stylized animated history channels likely do not. Disclosure does not limit audience or monetization eligibility. Non-disclosure risk: manual labels, removal, suspension from YPP.
