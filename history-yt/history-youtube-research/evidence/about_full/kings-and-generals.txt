🎥 Watch 250+ exclusive videos on Youtube ► https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCMma... and Patreon ► https://www.patreon.com/kingsandgenerals

This season on Kings and Generals, members and patrons will watch how the Soviet Union stopped the German army’s seemingly unstoppable advance and began counter-attacking. They will see how the heirs of William the Conqueror fought each other, plunging England into the Anarchy. They will witness Europe descend into the War of the Austrian Succession, igniting another “World War Zero” across the globe. They will explore the struggle for Sicily before the rise of Imperial Rome, as the Greeks and Carthaginians fought for dominance. They will follow Alexios and his heirs as they launched the Komnenian Restoration of the Eastern Roman Empire against the backdrop of Turkish expansion and the Crusades. They will also see how the Bolsheviks won the bloody and ruthless Russian Civil War.

In addition, we are launching new exclusive series on ancient Roman and Greek history, alongside long-form videos on the Second Punic War, the Russo-Japanese War, the War of the Spanish Succession, the Iberian Reconquista, and the Italian Unification Wars. Many new and continuing public series are also in development.

In a world where AI is increasingly taking over YouTube and undermining history channels, Kings and Generals vows to continue releasing three public and two exclusive videos every week. You can support us on this challenging road by becoming a member or patron via the links in the description, or by pressing the Join button under this video.

You can own a coin bearing the portrait of Julius Caesar, whose lifetime portrait coinage broke with Roman Republican tradition. You can hold a tetradrachm of Alexander the Great, struck as his empire spread across the ancient world. You can even collect coins bearing Cleopatra VII and Mark Antony together. Whether you love politics, warfare, mythology, sports, astrology, religion or art, there is probably an ancient coin connected to it. Coinage emerged in western Anatolia around the 7th century BC and spread throughout the Mediterranean and beyond. These coins were struck individually by hand and traveled with soldiers, merchants and ordinary people. Some were countermarked or struck over older coins and returned to circulation. That history is still visible on them today. The hobby can seem enormous, but you don’t have to learn everything before buying your first coin. Hey all! Unfortunately, despite our best efforts, we will have to make the Eastern Front series exclusive to the YouTube members and patrons. Why? Because despite the fact that those videos are more expensive to make and require a larger team, we only get half as many views for them than for our other videos. It’s difficult to explain, but YouTube tends to segment channels into niches. And no matter how many World War II videos we make and release, our videos just don’t get promoted to the audience interested in that topic. The problem is not even that we get so few views for that series in comparison to our other videos. In a vacuum, we would just eat the costs. It is the fact that each unsuccessful video moves the channel further down in YouTube's algorithm, and in search and discovery. Each video that is not watched means that the channel is worse off. It is ok when that is an odd video outside of a series or one that is part of a short series, but Eastern Front will have around 200 episodes, and we can't allow a series that long to drag the entire channel. By the time it is said and done, the entire channel might be dead. Past experience has shown that making lower-viewed series exclusive increases the views across the channel. After we continued our Pacific War series as an exclusive, the views have increased by 30-40% for the channel. That is significant. The member and patron videos are treated separately by the algorithm, they do not affect the algorithm in any way, so continuing the series in this fashion makes perfect sense for the health of the channel. We know that it is a tough pill to swallow, but, trust us, it is the same for us, too. We will continue releasing the Eastern Front podcasts publicly - https://www.youtube.com/@KNGPodcast. These podcasts are on average twice as long as the videos and are packed with extra info and details, making them a better proposition in terms of educational value. We also noticed that the only longer/combined Eastern Front video that we released did really well in terms of views, so after we are done with releasing long Pacific War videos (planning at least once a month for the next while), we will start releasing longer Eastern Front videos to ensure that you can see as much of the series as you can. Our members and patrons get 3 new exclusive videos per week, and can now watch more than 230 exclusive videos not available anywhere else. They also get early access to all videos, access to the schedule and our exclusive discord, voting rights and much more. Join them here (https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCMma...) and here (https://www.patreon.com/c/kingsandgen.... Thank you for understanding. This decision was made to ensure the long term health of the channel, but we are still sorry.
