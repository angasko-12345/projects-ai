## Evidence labels

Throughout the docs: `VERIFIED` = extracted or read from a primary source on
2026-11-05; `ESTIMATE` = derived approximation (method stated); `UNKNOWN` = not
verifiable with available tools; `HYPOTHESIS` = testable reading of public data,
not private analytics.
